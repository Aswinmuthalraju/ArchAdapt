## 1. The Core Value Proposition: The "Dual-Audience" Bridge

Traditional architectural software forces an unacceptable compromise:
- **Homeowners** are shut out by the extreme complexity of Revit and AutoCAD, left with crude 2D sketching tools or static AI image generators that create unbuildable dream houses.
- **Architects** waste dozens of hours drafting repetitive schematic variations, redrawing walls to test client changes, and explaining zoning limits manually.

**ArchAdapt AI bridges this gap through a unified platform:**

## 3. High-Priority Product Differentiation Commitments for Agent 1

For this transformation release, Agent 1 will execute the following foundational capabilities:

1. **Interactive 3D CAD & Cutaway System:**
   - Clickable rooms in 3D with hover outlines and real-time dimension badges.
   - Horizontal section cutaway mode (cutting plane) exposing internal room layouts.
   - Elevation camera view buttons (Front, Rear, Left, Right, Iso, Top).
   - Selection synchronization with the 2D floor plan.

2. **Professional 2D Blueprint Standard:**
   - Architectural graphic scale bar (showing 0, 5, 10, 20 ft increments).
   - Interior dimension witness lines between room walls.
   - Dual-unit room tags (sq ft & sq m) and legible typography.
   - Monolithic wall poche fills and door jamb stops.

3. **Surgical, Contextual What-If Mutations:**
   - Contextual room action bar: When a room is selected in 2D or 3D, instant quick-action buttons appear ("Enlarge Room", "Reduce Room", "Convert to Ensuite", "Change Color").
   - Direct execution of surgical room area mutations that adjust neighboring boundaries without scrambling the entire floor plan.

4. **Cohesive Internal Workspace UI:**
   - Eliminates disconnected tabs; brings 2D, 3D, and contextual property inspection into unified harmony.
   - Clean, professional design language that elevates ArchAdapt to a commercial-grade tool.
